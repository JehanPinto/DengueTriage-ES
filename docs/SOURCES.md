# Sources of the Knowledge Base

Every rule in `src/kb.pl` was transcribed from one of the published guidelines listed below. **No rule was written from the developer's own judgement, and none was generated by a language model.** Each rule in the code carries a `source/3` fact naming the document and the page, and the automated test suite fails if any rule lacks one.

No domain expert was interviewed. As permitted by the assignment brief, the domain knowledge was taken from reliable published sources instead, and each is cited below.

## Other sources consulted

- <https://ccp.lk/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Guidelines-on-Management-of-DF-DHF-in-Adults.pdf>

## Askable facts (the questions the system can ask)

**A. Exposure and fever**

- `endemic_exposure` — Does the patient live in, or has travelled in the last 14 days to, a dengue-endemic area?
- `fever` — Does the patient have fever, or a history of fever in this illness?
- `lab_confirmed` — Is there a positive laboratory test for dengue (NS1 antigen, IgM or RT-PCR)?

**B. Probable-dengue criteria**

- `nausea_vomiting` — Nausea or vomiting?
- `rash` — Rash?
- `aches_pains` — Aches and pains (headache, retro-orbital pain, myalgia, arthralgia)?
- `tourniquet_positive` — Is the tourniquet test positive?
- `leukopenia` — Leukopenia (low total white cell count)?

**C. Warning signs**

- `abdominal_pain` — Abdominal pain or tenderness?
- `persistent_vomiting` — Persistent vomiting?
- `fluid_accumulation` — Clinical fluid accumulation (pleural effusion or ascites)?
- `mucosal_bleeding` — Mucosal bleeding (gums, nose, vaginal or gastrointestinal)?
- `lethargy_restlessness` — Lethargy or restlessness?
- `liver_enlargement` — Liver enlargement greater than 2 cm?
- `hct_rising` — Is the haematocrit rising?
- `platelet_dropping` — Is the platelet count falling rapidly?

**D. Signs of severe dengue**

- `narrow_pulse_pressure` — Is the pulse pressure 20 mmHg or less?
- `poor_perfusion` — Signs of poor capillary perfusion (cold extremities, delayed capillary refill or rapid pulse)?
- `hypotension` — Is the patient hypotensive (systolic blood pressure has fallen)?
- `respiratory_distress` — Respiratory distress?
- `overt_bleeding_unstable` — Persistent or severe overt bleeding with unstable haemodynamic status?
- `hct_drop_after_resuscitation` — Has the haematocrit fallen after fluid resuscitation while the patient remains haemodynamically unstable?
- `ast_alt_ge_1000` — Is AST or ALT 1000 IU/L or above?
- `impaired_consciousness` — Impaired consciousness?
- `heart_impairment` — Impairment of the heart or other organs?

**E. Co-existing conditions and social circumstances**

- `pregnancy` — Is the patient pregnant?
- `infancy` — Is the patient an infant?
- `old_age` — Is the patient elderly?
- `obesity` — Is the patient obese?
- `diabetes_mellitus` — Diabetes mellitus?
- `renal_failure` — Renal failure?
- `chronic_haemolytic_disease` — Chronic haemolytic disease (for example thalassaemia)?
- `lives_alone` — Does the patient live alone?
- `lives_far_from_facility` — Does the patient live far from a health facility without reliable transport?

**F. Oral intake, urine output, haematocrit**

- `tolerates_oral_fluids` — Can the patient tolerate adequate volumes of oral fluids?
- `passes_urine_6h` — Does the patient pass urine at least once every six hours?
- `stable_haematocrit` — Is the haematocrit stable?

**G. Phase of illness**

- `defervescence` — Has the temperature dropped to 37.5 C or below and stayed there (defervescence)?
- `day_of_illness_3_7` — Is this day 3 to 7 of the illness?

**H. Discharge assessment**

- `afebrile_48h` — Has the patient been free of fever for 48 hours?
- `clinical_improvement` — Is there improvement in well-being, appetite, haemodynamic status and urine output, with no respiratory distress?
- `platelet_rising` — Is the platelet count showing an increasing trend?
